This page is where CleaningApp finishes signing you in

If you are seeing this in a browser, the app did not pick the address up. That normally means CleaningApp is not installed on this device, or it is installed but the link has not been verified yet.

Nothing has gone wrong with your account, and there is nothing to do on this page. Open CleaningApp and sign in from there.

This address exists so the operating system can hand the sign-in result straight to the app rather than asking you to confirm which app should receive it.
